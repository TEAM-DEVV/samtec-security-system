# 11 · Client presentation plan

Two audiences, one build. Examiners are convinced by **rigour**; the client is convinced by **losses stopped**. Same demo, different framing.

## The 15-minute client demo

Rehearse in Phase 6, deliver in Phase 8.

1. **Open on the pain (2 minutes):** "Every month you pay people. How many of them actually stood a post? Today you cannot prove it. This system makes it provable."
2. **Enroll (3 minutes):** register a new guard live at the face kiosk, the wow moment. Show the consent step, which signals professionalism.
3. **Clock in and watch the dashboard (2 minutes):** the guard clocks in, the dashboard updates within seconds, and the site board shows who is on post right now.
4. **Payroll run (4 minutes):** close the seeded month, create a draft, approve it as a second user to show maker–checker, open a payslip PDF and the bank export file.
5. **The decisive moment (3 minutes):** run the detection sweep live. Three planted ghosts appear with their evidence: an employee who never clocked in, one face enrolled under two names, and a guard "at two sites at once". Then say: "This is the money you stop losing every month."
6. **Close (1 minute):** a pilot offer: one site, one month, their real roster. Success means ghosts found or attendance verified.

## Objections we will hear (prepare the answers now)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Guards will break or avoid the device." | PIN fallback exists but is flagged and reported (rule R7). Avoiding the device becomes a signal in itself. |
| "There is no internet at the posts." | Devices store punches offline and sync later, so nothing is lost. Show it: unplug the network mid-demo, then plug it back in. |
| "What about our current payroll in Excel?" | We import the employee list from CSV, and the bank export matches their current format. |
| "Is fingerprint data safe and legal?" | Templates, not images; encrypted; consent recorded; a retention policy under Act 843. Hand over a one-page data policy. |
| "What does it cost?" | A free or low-cost pilot, then a monthly fee per active employee plus a one-time device cost. Decide the numbers before the meeting; never improvise prices on stage. |

## Deliverables for the meeting

- A live, hosted demo (never localhost in front of a client), seeded, with a script for the planted ghosts
- A one-page PDF: the problem, the solution, the security approach and the pilot offer
